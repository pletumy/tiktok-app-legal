Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data TikTok Content Manager ("the Service") accesses when you connect your TikTok account, and how that data is used.

1. Data We Access

When you authorize the Service via TikTok Login Kit, we access only the data covered by the scopes you approve, which may include:

ScopeData accessed
user.info.profileProfile link, deep link, bio description, verification status
user.info.statsFollower count, following count, likes count, video count
video.listList of your public videos
video.publishAbility to publish content to your account on your behalf

2. How We Use This Data

Data accessed is used solely to provide the Service's features: displaying your connected accounts, showing basic performance stats, and scheduling/publishing content that you create and approve.

3. Data We Do Not Do

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your TikTok data for advertising purposes. We do not access private messages, as TikTok does not provide API access to direct messages.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access tokens and refresh tokens are stored encrypted and used only to make authorized API calls on your behalf. You may revoke access at any time by disconnecting your account in the Service or through your TikTok account settings.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains connected to the Service, plus a limited period afterward for operational logs, after which it is deleted.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with TikTok's official APIs and may integrate with other services you explicitly connect (such as Canva) to support content creation. Data is only shared with these services as needed to perform the actions you request.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting us. You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time to stop further data access.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
